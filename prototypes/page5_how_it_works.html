How it works
The plain-language version lives on the rest of the site. This page is for anyone curious about the logic underneath — the same math and rules that power every diagnosis, shown with real numbers from the data currently in this browser.
The core idea
Every reading compares a piece only to that creator's own past pieces — never to other creators or an industry average. Different accounts have different follower counts, prices, and audiences, so an outside benchmark would mostly measure the wrong thing. TikTok and RedNote each keep a separate baseline, and boosted pieces never mix with organic ones, since paid reach isn't earned by the content.
Why this mattersA piece isn't "good" or "bad" in the abstract — it's only above or below what's normal for that specific creator, on that specific platform, under the same boost conditions.
Right now, in this browser
These numbers are computed live from whatever pieces exist in local storage — seed examples plus anything entered through the form or bulk import.
The seven rules
A diagnosis never free-writes a reason. It locates the weakest stage of the funnel, then checks a fixed set of seven rules against that stage's evidence. Each rule has fired at least once in the current dataset — the count on the right is how many pieces triggered it, right now.
Confidence, expressed honestly
A reading is only as good as how much history backs it. Rather than hide that, the confidence label says so directly.
