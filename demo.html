Creator Campaign Review — Live Demo

This page runs the real analysis engine in your browser. It loads the demo dataset, builds the creator's personalized baseline, and computes the diagnosis live. Nothing here is hard coded. Change the numbers and the diagnosis changes with them.

1. Why the reading window is day 2 (decided by data, not by preference)

The order conversion rate is measured on the same piece at day 1, 2, 3, and 7. The bars show how much the rate changes from one reading to the next. The change collapses after day 2, which is what makes day 2 the earliest point the rate can be trusted.
